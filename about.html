About Us

Information Governance, done properly

360 Governance provides specialist Information Governance and Data Protection Officer services, built specifically around the needs of primary care.

Who we are

360 Governance is a specialist Information Governance and Data Protection Officer service, focused entirely on primary care. We work with GP practices, Primary Care Networks and Federations to help them manage information effectively, meet their regulatory responsibilities and protect patient data.

With over 10 years of healthcare experience behind our work, we understand the day-to-day pressures primary care organisations face — and we build our support around that reality, rather than a one-size-fits-all compliance checklist.
